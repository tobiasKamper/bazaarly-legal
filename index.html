Bazaarly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.bazaarly

The short version: Your watchlist, order details, portfolio and settings are stored on your device. Bazaarly has no accounts and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. Online requests for prices, charts and some item icons send your IP address and request details to Hypixel or Coflnet, as explained in section 4. You can also export your local data to a file you choose. If you send feedback by email, the developer receives your email address and message (section 6).

You can open this policy at any time inside the app under Settings → About → Privacy policy. Section 11 lists the sources of the data Bazaarly shows and the licenses that apply to them.

1. Who is responsible

Bazaarly is a non-commercial hobby project developed and maintained by a private individual.

  • Developer: Tobias Kamper, Austria
  • Contact: tobias2kamper@gmail.com

2. Accounts and analytics

Bazaarly has no user accounts or registration and does not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. The app does not upload your local database to the developer or the price-data providers. This does not mean that using the app sends no data over the internet: the providers receive connection and request information when the app loads online content, as described in section 4.

3. Data you create in the app

Bazaarly stores your watchlist, price triggers, open bazaar orders, portfolio holdings and app settings in a database on your device. It does not automatically upload these records to the developer or the API providers. Item IDs requested for charts or missing icons are described separately in section 4.

Deleting local data

Settings → Reset all data deletes your watchlist (including its price triggers), entries and portfolio. Your app settings are kept, including your language, theme, list display and automatic refresh interval.

To remove the app's locally stored data including these settings, clear Bazaarly's app storage in Android's system settings or uninstall the app. Resetting, clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete backup files you previously saved outside the app or any request logs held by external providers.

Export and import

Export writes your watchlist, entries, portfolio and settings to a JSON file. Android's file picker lets you choose where to save it. Import reads a backup file you select. A location offered by the file picker may be on your device or with a cloud storage provider you have configured. If you select a cloud location, that provider handles the file under its own policies.

Keep exported files private if you do not want others to read their contents. Delete exported backups separately from their saved location, including any copies or cloud backups you have made. The app's reset function does not remove them.

4. Network connections

Bazaarly uses HTTPS requests to load current prices, price history and item icons that are not bundled with the app. These requests go to the following providers:

ServicePurposeRequest details
api.hypixel.net
Hypixel		 Current bazaar buy and sell prices A request to the public bazaar endpoint. The app supplies no API key or account details.
sky.coflnet.com
Coflnet / SkyCofl		 Historical price data and missing item icons The ID of the item being displayed and, for price history, the selected time range.

In both cases, the receiving service obtains your IP address, the requested resource and the technical headers sent with the request. An IP address can be personal data. HTTPS protects the connection in transit; it does not hide these details from the service receiving the request.

Both providers are independent services. They process these requests under their own privacy policies and as their own controllers; Bazaarly does not instruct them and the developer does not receive copies of their logs through the app.

  • Coflnet GmbH (Germany), operator of SkyCofl, states in its privacy policy (section 4.1, version of 30 September 2026) that its server logs contain the IP address, date and time, requested URL, HTTP status, transferred data volume, referrer and browser/operating-system information. Coflnet uses them to deliver the service, to detect and block abuse and to bill infrastructure services, and keeps centrally collected access logs for no more than seven days.
  • Hypixel Inc. describes the technical and usage data it processes, including IP addresses, in its privacy policy. It does not state a separate retention period for anonymous requests to its public API.

These API requests do not upload your saved watchlist records, trigger prices, order quantities, purchase prices, portfolio balances or settings. Coflnet does receive the item IDs requested for the charts or icons you view, together with the connection information described above. Resetting your local app data does not delete provider logs.

Item descriptions and recipes are bundled with the app (section 11), so viewing them sends no request. The "Open in wiki" button in an item's Info view opens the item's page, or a search for its name, on the Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki (hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki) in your browser, and only when you tap it. The app itself sends nothing to the wiki; the wiki's own privacy policy applies there.

5. Permissions

The only permission Bazaarly asks Android for is INTERNET, needed to load prices, charts and missing item icons. You are never shown a permission prompt.

The app package also declares the internal permission com.bazaarly.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is added automatically by Google's AndroidX libraries, can only be used by Bazaarly itself and gives no access to any of your data. Bazaarly does not request access to storage, contacts, location, camera, microphone or any other sensitive resource.

6. Feedback by email

Settings → About → Give feedback can open your own email app with a prefilled message to the developer. The message includes the Bazaarly version and your Android version so that bug reports can be reproduced. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

If you send it, the developer receives your email address, your message and anything you attach. They are used only to read and answer your feedback, are kept in the developer's mailbox (Google Gmail) for as long as needed for that and are deleted on request. They are not shared with anyone else. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7. Donations

The app contains an optional link to a PayPal donation page. The app itself processes no payments and receives no payment data. Following that link opens PayPal in your browser, where PayPal's own privacy statement applies. A donation does not unlock anything in the app. Bazaarly is free, contains no ads and has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

8. Children

Bazaarly is not directed at children under 13. The local storage and network behaviour described above apply to anyone using the app.

9. Your rights and managing your data

You can manage your locally stored records in the app and export them to a backup file. Section 3 explains how to delete records, remove settings and delete exported backups. The developer cannot use the app to access or erase the database on your device remotely.

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your personal data, to data portability and to object to processing. For requests concerning data the developer holds (for example a feedback email), contact the developer using the address in section 1. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde.

For request logs held by Hypixel or Coflnet, including retention and deletion options, contact the respective provider. The app's local reset cannot act on those logs, and the absence of an account or analytics does not mean that no personal data is processed by external services.

10. Changes to this policy

Should a future version of Bazaarly change how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change accordingly.

11. Data sources and licenses

Bazaarly shows data from these sources:

  • Current bazaar prices: the public Hypixel API (api.hypixel.net).
  • Price history and item icons that are not bundled with the app: SkyCofl by Coflnet (sky.coflnet.com).
  • Item descriptions, recipes and the "Used in" list in an item's Info view: the NotEnoughUpdates-REPO. This data is bundled with the app and refreshed with each release.

The "Open in wiki" button links to the Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki; no wiki texts are included in the app.
